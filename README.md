## ✨ About the Project

**ZChecklist** is a school project built around a simple idea: managing checklists should be effortless, intuitive, and visually delightful.

Designed with a premium, futuristic aesthetic, ZChecklist transforms a traditional numbered checklist into an interactive experience. Whether you're tracking completed entries, identifying unfinished ones, or generating a final summary, everything is organized in one place.

The interface combines vibrant purple-blue gradients, translucent glass surfaces, soft lighting, rounded elements, and fluid transitions to create a clean and immersive experience.

The goal? To make something useful feel absolutely beautiful. 💜

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🌈 **Multilingual Welcome Screen** — A welcoming greeting that cycles through 10 languages.
- 🔢 **Dynamic Checklist Generation** — Generate numbered entries based on the total number of members you need to check.
- ✅ **Done & Not Done Tracking** — Mark entries with clear visual status indicators.
- 🗑️ **Safe Deletion** — Confirm deletions while preserving deleted entries for the final report.
- 💾 **Automatic Progress Saving** — Save checklist progress in your browser and restore it after refreshing.
- 📊 **Interactive Final Report** — View totals for completed, unfinished, deleted, and unmarked entries.
- 🔍 **Expandable Details** — Expand report sections to reveal the exact entry numbers in each category.
- 📥 **Downloadable Reports** — Export checklist summaries and entry-number lists as a plain-text `.txt` file.
- 🔄 **Resume or Start Fresh** — Choose whether to continue a saved checklist or begin a new one.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Adapt the interface to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- 🫧 **Liquid Glass-Inspired UI** — Enjoy translucent panels, soft gradients, rounded cards, and smooth transitions.
- 🎨 **Animated Aurora Background** — A purple-blue atmosphere with beautiful green and violet light effects.

## 💡 How It Works

### 1. 🌈 Welcome

Open ZChecklist and enjoy a greeting that cycles through different languages.

### 2. 🔢 Set Up Your Checklist

Enter the total number of members you need to check, and ZChecklist generates the numbered entries for you.

### 3. ✅ Track Each Entry

Click an entry to mark it as **Done**, **Not Done**, or **Delete** it with confirmation.

Each status has its own visual appearance, making progress easy to understand at a glance.

### 4. 🏁 Complete Your Checklist

Finish the checklist whenever you're ready. If some active entries remain unmarked, ZChecklist warns you before proceeding.

### 5. 📊 Review Your Report

Explore your final report, including completed entries, unfinished entries, deleted entries, and unmarked entries.

Expand each section to see the exact entry numbers.

### 6. 📥 Download Your Report

Download a text file containing the report's date and time, summary statistics, totals, and relevant entry-number lists.

## 💾 Data & Privacy

ZChecklist uses browser-based storage to save checklist progress.

- 💾 Your progress can be restored after refreshing the page in the same browser.
- 🔐 No account is required for the core checklist experience.
- 📊 Reports are generated directly in the browser.
- 📄 Downloaded reports are saved through your browser's normal download process.

**Important:** Browser storage is tied to your browser and device. Clearing site data or switching browsers or devices may make saved progress unavailable. Automatic saving does not mean cloud synchronization.
